
# Easy Note 📝

**Organize Your Thoughts. Simplify Your Life.**

Easy Note is a modern, elegant, and feature-rich personal productivity application designed to help you organize notes, manage daily tasks, track important events, and stay productive.

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Smart Notes** – Create, edit, organize, and manage important notes.
- ✅ **Task Management** – Plan daily activities and track task completion.
- 📅 **Google Calendar Integration** – Synchronize important events and schedules.
- ⏰ **Date & Time Management** – Organize notes and events based on specific dates and times.
- 🔔 **Event Reminders** – Stay informed about upcoming tasks and important events.
- 🗂️ **Smart Organization** – Keep your notes, tasks, and events structured.
- 🎨 **Modern UI** – Enjoy a clean, elegant, and user-friendly interface.

## 🎯 Purpose

Easy Note aims to make everyday planning easier by bringing notes, tasks, and event management together in one convenient application.
